The applicants to GSoC are evaluated based on several metrics.

These metrics include,

1. Understanding of the project idea, as established in the project proposal, prior GitHub discussions, and other communications with the mentors.

   Please note your project proposal still carries the most weight. There is a minor caveat. A well-written last-minute proposal out of nowhere (i.e., mentors have never seen you in GitHub before) is unlikely to be ranked.

2. Successful completion of the code challenges (if any), prior contributions (pull requests/fixes) to the project, or related previous experience.

   It is important to complete any mandatory code challenge. It is also appreciated when an applicant establishes their understanding of the project through a PR. However, in the AI-assisted coding world, it is critical to note that the "numbers game" does not help much. Your goal should **not** be to contribute 100 PRs and complete 75% of the project during the application period.

3. Contributions to the project communications (such as reviewing other PRs, productive contributions to discussions, and overall community spirit).

   We value the open-source community spirit, as established through our GitHub forums, including assisting other applicants and newbie developers. The AI-assisted coding has shifted the burden from coding to reviewing and maintenance. As such, proactively collaborating and maintaining the project/community through testing and discussions are welcome! Please note that the GSoC is still an individual work. As such, your proposal shouldn't be written as if you are aiming for a team work for a given project idea. Only one contributor can be accepted for a project idea.
   
5. NOT abusing AI.
  
   While we appreciate PRs (AI-assisted or not), a sudden surge of 20 PRs with a wall-of-text response to mentor/developer queries are considered a negative contribution. We want to bring in human contributors. If your response to a simple question is a nicely copy-pasted ChatGPT/Gemini response, you are likely to be ranked lower. Same with hallucinated text and references. We do not want to be the first to review an AI slop of a text. Quality >> Quantity, when it comes to PRs and contributions.

6. Your CV and experience. 

   This includes open source contributions to other Alaska projects (other than through a previous GSoC. A prior GSoC experience with Alaska is not counted) and open source contributions to other organizations (which can be highlighted through GitHub links). Strength of the applicant bio is indeed critical. While we appreciate your ability to learn to code, prior experience on the language and toolkit is still highly valued. We do not want you to get stuck on a trivial matter just because the AI agent gave you a conflicting opinion.

7. Ability to contribute and commit the specified time frame (i.e., 350 hours for the full-time project, 175 hours for the medium projects, and 90 hours for small projects).

   Pls also pay attention to the mentors' timezones. If you indicate your preferred work-time is 10 pm - 6 am in the primary mentor's timezone, you will fit in another project where your working time aligns better with the mentor.

8. Willingness and capacity to work with Alaska beyond the GSoC timeframe as collaborators, committers, and future GSoC mentors.

    This is sadly a difficult metric since almost everyone mentions they will stick around beyond the GSoC. ;) But we surely appreciate contributor-turned-mentors.

9. Alignment with our vision "Many Traditions, One Alaska" as established through the GSoC Proposal and GitHub Discussions.

   While we have project ideas from organizations beyond Alaska, our projects are centered around Alaska and the "Many Traditions, One Alaska" motto of the University of Alaska. We want our contributors to maintain that friendly and welcoming environment. Please note that GSoC is competitive. Oftentimes, Google offers organizations fewer slots than we asked for. We will try to mentor as many projects as possible. In 2026, we asked for 12 slots and received only 8 from Google. If you are not accepted, please do not feel discouraged. We still appreciated your contributions.
